# Code of conduct

LiteDIC is a small research project. Everyone taking part in it - in issues,
pull requests, discussions or e-mail - is expected to:

- be respectful and constructive, and assume good intent;
- criticise ideas and code, not people;
- not harass, insult or discriminate against anyone for any reason;
- respect other people's time: give enough detail to reproduce a problem.

Unacceptable behaviour can be reported to the maintainer at
iliiawuest@gmail.com. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may
remove comments, close issues or block participants who do not follow these
rules.
